# 🛒 Flipkart Sales Report – Interactive Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Title / Headline  
**Flipkart Sales Report – Interactive Sales Performance Dashboard**

An interactive sales analytics dashboard that helps analyze Flipkart’s sales performance, profit distribution, customer demographics, and regional trends through visual data insights.

---

## 🎯 Short Description / Purpose  
This project provides a visual analysis of Flipkart’s retail sales data using an interactive dashboard.

The purpose of the dashboard is to understand overall sales performance, profit contribution, customer behavior, and product trends across different zones, categories, age groups, and delivery types.

The dashboard enables faster decision-making by transforming raw sales data into clear and actionable visual insights.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Used to design and develop the interactive dashboard  
- 🔄 **Power Query** – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation  
- 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used to calculate measures such as total sales, average sales, total profit, and item count  
- 🧩 **Data Modeling** – Relationships built between sales, product, customer, and location data  
- 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` (Power BI report file)  

---

## 📂 Data Source  

**Source:** Flipkart Sales Dataset(raw data).xlsx  

### 📄 Dataset Description  
The dataset contains detailed information related to Flipkart’s sales operations, including:

- 💰 Sales amount and profit  
- 📦 Number of items sold  
- 🏷️ Product categories and sub-categories  
- 👤 Customer demographics (gender and age group)  
- 🚚 Delivery types (Standard, Express, Shipped from Store)  
- 🌍 Sales zones and locations  
- 📅 Monthly and yearly sales records 

The data was cleaned and structured using **Power Query** before visualization to ensure accuracy and consistency.

---

## ✨ Features / Highlights  

### 🔹 Business Problem  
E-commerce companies like Flipkart generate huge volumes of sales data daily. However, without proper visualization, it becomes difficult to:

- Track sales and profit performance  
- Compare product categories and sub-categories  
- Understand customer demographics  
- Identify high-performing regions and zones  

Raw data alone does not provide quick or actionable insights.

---

### 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard  
The main goals of this dashboard are to:

- Provide a high-level view of Flipkart’s sales and profit performance  
- Analyze monthly sales trends  
- Compare performance across products, zones, genders, and age groups  
- Support business decisions related to marketing, inventory, and logistics  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Walkthrough  

### 📌 Key Performance Indicators (Top Section)  
- 💵 **Total Sales:** $136M  
- 💹 **Total Profit:** $12.8M  
- 📊 **Average Sales:** $1.2K  
- 📦 **Number of Items Sold:** 113K  

These KPIs provide a quick summary of overall business performance.

---

### 📈 Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)  
Displays and compares total sales by month, helping identify high and low-performing sales on monthly basis throughout the year.

---

### 🍩 Product Sales Distribution (Donut Chart)  
Shows total sales contribution based on major product categories. Help indentify best performing products on an overall basis.

---

### 📊 Sales by Sub-Category (Bar Chart)  
Highlights top-performing sub-categories including Vitamins, Medical Supplies, Fashion items, etc.

---

### 👥 Sales by Gender (Bar Chart)  
Compares sales generated by male and female customers across different product categories.

---

### 🌍 Sales by Zone (Horizontal Bar Chart)  
Displays sales contribution by zone allowing to optimize resource allocation and identify specific market trends.

---

### 🧩 Product Sales by Zone (Grouped Bar Chart)  
Compares category-wise sales performance across multiple zones to understand performance differences caused by local demographics, competition, or seasonal trends.

---

### 🚚 Sales by Delivery Type (Pie Chart)  
Analyzes sales distribution across Standard Delivery, Shipped from Store, and Express Delivery essential for optimizing operational efficiency, controlling costs, and meeting customer expectations.

---

### 👶🧓 Sales by Age and Gender (Bar Chart)  
Breaks down sales by customer age groups and gender to better understand customer demographics.

---

## 🎛️ Filters / Slicers  

- 📅 Year  
- 📍 Location  
- 🏷️ Product Category  

These filters allow users to interactively explore data based on specific criteria.

---

## 💡 Insights & Business Impact (Overall)

- 📍 **Zone 3** generates the highest sales, indicating strong regional demand  
- 👗 Health and Beauty products contribute significantly to total revenue  
- 🚚 Standard, Shipped from Abroad and Express delivery types contribute nearly equal sales shares  
- 👥 The **30–40** and **20–30** age groups are the strongest customer segments  
- 🗓️ The window of January to May shows peak in sales indicating high seasonal trends

## 📑 Results / Outputs  

- A fully interactive Power BI sales dashboard  
- Clear visualization of sales, profit, and customer trends  
- Easy comparison across products, regions, and demographics  
- Actionable insights for business planning and strategic decision-making
